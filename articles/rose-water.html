Rose Water Mist: A Daily Intention for the Skin

Rose water has graced apothecary shelves for millennia. This two-ingredient mist is a moment of care you can carry with you.

What You'll Need

  • Distilled rose water — 4 oz
  • Vegetable glycerin — 1 tsp
  • Small spray bottle

The Ritual

Combine in the bottle, shake gently, and mist over your face after cleansing or anytime your skin feels dry. Store in the fridge for an extra-cooling touch.
