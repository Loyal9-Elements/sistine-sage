Fire Cider: The Folk Tonic That Bites Back

This potent vinegar infusion has roots in folk herbalism. It's sharp, warming, and made for the season when everyone around you is sniffling.

What You'll Need

  • Apple cider vinegar — 32 oz
  • Fresh horseradish root, grated
  • White onion, diced
  • Garlic cloves, crushed
  • Fresh ginger, sliced
  • Raw honey to taste

The Ritual

Combine all ingredients in a jar, cover with ACV, and steep for 4 weeks. Strain, stir in honey, and take a tablespoon daily — or whenever you need fire.
